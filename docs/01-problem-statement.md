# 01. Problem Statement: Jadoo

## 1. Product Overview

**Jadoo** is a real-estate decision-intelligence platform designed to help people relocating to unfamiliar cities discover, evaluate, compare, and shortlist rental properties.

The initial MVP focuses on people moving to **Bangalore** who need to find a suitable 1BHK rental without spending excessive time browsing listings, contacting agents, and manually comparing property details.

Jadoo aims to make rental decisions more transparent by bringing property information, total monthly costs, commute considerations, and virtual inspections into one workflow.

## 2. Problem Background

Finding a rental home in an unfamiliar city is a fragmented and time-consuming process. Prospective tenants often rely on multiple property portals, agent conversations, maps, and individual property visits to make a decision.

Important information, such as maintenance charges, security deposits, furnishing, locality, and commute time, may be incomplete or difficult to compare across listings.

Consequently, tenants struggle to determine which properties genuinely fit their budget, lifestyle, and location requirements.

## 3. Core Problem Statement

People relocating to Bangalore lack a unified and structured way to identify suitable rental properties and compare their actual costs, commute convenience, amenities, and physical suitability before arranging property visits.

This leads to inefficient searches, repeated manual comparisons, uncertainty about additional expenses, and potentially poor rental decisions.

## 4. Target Users

The initial target audience includes:

- **Working professionals:** People relocating to Bangalore for employment.
- **Students and fresh graduates:** People moving for education, internships, or their first jobs.
- **Early-career renters:** Individuals seeking affordable, convenient, and suitable accommodation.

## 5. Current Pain Points

1. **Fragmented discovery:** Listings and property information are spread across different platforms and sources.
2. **Unclear total costs:** Rent alone does not represent the complete monthly housing expense.
3. **Difficult comparisons:** Differences in furnishing, amenities, locality, and deposits make properties difficult to evaluate consistently.
4. **Commute uncertainty:** Tenants must independently investigate travel time to work, college, or public transport.
5. **Limited remote inspection:** Photos may not adequately communicate a property's layout and spatial characteristics.
6. **High search effort:** Repeated browsing, note-taking, agent communication, and physical visits consume time.

## 6. Proposed Solution

Jadoo will provide a structured rental discovery and evaluation experience that allows users to:

- Specify their budget, preferred locality, furnishing requirements, and commute preferences.
- Discover and filter suitable 1BHK rental properties.
- View standardized property details, amenities, deposits, and estimated recurring costs.
- Compare shortlisted properties side by side.
- Evaluate locality and commute information.
- Inspect supported properties through basic 360-degree or 3D virtual tours.
- Save shortlisted properties for further evaluation.

## 7. MVP Scope

### In Scope

- Rental property discovery and listing pages.
- Search and filtering based on user preferences.
- Property detail pages with standardized information.
- Estimated total monthly cost breakdown.
- Basic commute and locality evaluation.
- Side-by-side property comparison.
- Property shortlisting.
- Basic virtual property inspection where supported.
- Curated demo dataset for initial testing.

### Out of Scope

- Rental payments and booking transactions.
- Owner onboarding and broker management workflows.
- Legal verification and property ownership verification.
- Advanced machine-learning recommendations.
- AR-based navigation.
- AI chatbot functionality.

## 8. Product Hypothesis

If relocating tenants can evaluate rental properties using standardized cost breakdowns, commute information, comparable property details, and virtual inspections, they will be able to shortlist suitable properties with less effort and greater confidence.

This hypothesis must be validated through user research and measurable experiments.

## 9. Initial Success Criteria

The MVP should demonstrate that users can:

- Discover relevant properties using their stated preferences.
- Understand estimated recurring costs and upfront expenses.
- Compare multiple properties without manually compiling information.
- Shortlist properties that satisfy their primary requirements.
- Evaluate supported properties remotely before deciding whether to visit.

Quantitative targets will be established in the KPI Framework and Experiment Design documents after an initial baseline is collected.

## 10. Key Assumptions

- The initial use case is finding a 1BHK rental in Bangalore.
- Users value transparent cost information and commute convenience alongside rent.
- Standardized property data improves comparison quality.
- Virtual inspections can reduce uncertainty before physical visits.
- A curated dataset is sufficient to validate the MVP workflow before scaling inventory.

## 11. Open Questions

- Which property attributes most strongly influence shortlisting?
- How should estimated monthly costs account for variable utilities and maintenance?
- Which commute mode and destination should be supported first?
- Do virtual tours materially improve users' confidence?
- How much does structured comparison reduce the time required to shortlist properties?

## 12. Problem Statement Summary

Jadoo addresses the difficulty of finding and evaluating rental homes in unfamiliar cities by consolidating property discovery, cost transparency, commute evaluation, comparison, shortlisting, and virtual inspection into a single decision-making workflow.

The MVP will validate whether this structured experience helps relocating tenants make faster and more informed rental decisions.
