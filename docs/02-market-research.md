# 02. Market Research: Jadoo

## 1. Research Objective

Understand the Bangalore rental housing market, identify the problems faced by people searching for rental properties, evaluate existing alternatives, and identify opportunities for Jadoo's MVP.

**Research scope:** Rental housing in Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

**Primary audience:** Students, fresh graduates, and working professionals relocating to Bangalore.

**Research approach:** Secondary research, competitor analysis, and primary user validation.

## 2. Market Overview

Bangalore is an important technology and startup hub that attracts working professionals, entrepreneurs, and students. Rental housing demand is influenced by employment locations, educational institutions, transport connectivity, affordability, and residential infrastructure.

Magicbricks' January-March 2026 Rental Index reported an increase in rental demand in Bangalore and substantial differences in average rents across popular localities. These differences reinforce the importance of considering locality and budget together rather than evaluating rent in isolation.

**Source:** [Magicbricks Rental Index](https://property.magicbricks.com/microsite/research-insights/rental-index/index.html)

### Relevant Market Characteristics

- **Location-sensitive demand:** Proximity to employment hubs and public transportation influences property selection.
- **Budget constraints:** Rent, deposits, maintenance, and brokerage can affect affordability.
- **Fragmented discovery:** Renters use property portals, owner listings, agents, maps, and personal networks.
- **Information asymmetry:** Listing details may not provide enough information to evaluate a property before visiting.
- **Digital discovery:** Online platforms allow tenants to browse and shortlist properties remotely.

These characteristics establish a relevant problem space for Jadoo. They do not, by themselves, prove that users will adopt a new platform.

## 3. Target Market Segments

### Segment A: Relocating Working Professionals

People moving to Bangalore for a new job or changing their workplace.

**Primary needs**
- Find housing near the office.
- Understand the complete monthly housing cost.
- Reduce physical visits before relocation.
- Compare localities and commute options.

**Potential value:** High, particularly when the joining date is approaching.

### Segment B: Students and Fresh Graduates

People relocating for college, internships, training, or their first job.

**Primary needs**
- Find affordable accommodation.
- Understand deposits and recurring expenses.
- Identify convenient and accessible localities.
- Compare independent flats and other accommodation options.

**Potential value:** High, although affordability may be a major constraint.

### Segment C: Early-Career Professionals

People already living in Bangalore who are searching for a better rental home.

**Primary needs**
- Compare rent and amenities.
- Reduce commute time.
- Find better-value properties.
- Evaluate alternatives before moving.

**Potential value:** Moderate to high, depending on the urgency of the move.

### Initial Target Segment

Jadoo should initially focus on **working professionals and fresh graduates relocating to Bangalore who are searching for a 1BHK rental**.

This segment provides a specific use case for validating search, comparison, cost transparency, and remote inspection.

## 4. Customer Problems and Research Hypotheses

| Customer problem | Research hypothesis | Validation method |
|---|---|---|
| Too many listings to evaluate | Structured filters reduce search effort | Usability testing |
| Unclear total expenses | Cost breakdown improves decision confidence | Prototype interviews |
| Difficulty comparing properties | Side-by-side comparison improves shortlisting | Task completion study |
| Uncertainty about commute | Commute information affects property preferences | User interviews |
| Inadequate remote inspection | Virtual tours improve confidence before visits | Controlled usability test |
| Outdated or incomplete listings | Freshness and completeness influence trust | Listing audit and interviews |

These are hypotheses, not established research findings. They must be tested with actual renters.

## 6. Competitor Landscape

### NoBroker

Website: https://www.nobroker.in/

**Core proposition:** Property discovery without traditional brokerage.

**Relevant capabilities**
- Rental search and property filters.
- Owner-direct listings.
- Property information and photographs.
- Shortlisting before physical visits.

**Implication for Jadoo:** Avoid competing only on listing discovery. Investigate whether a more structured comparison and decision workflow provides additional value.

### Magicbricks

Website: https://www.magicbricks.com/

**Core proposition:** Large-scale property discovery supported by listing inventory and real-estate research.

**Relevant capabilities**
- Rental property search.
- Locality-based discovery.
- Property listings and photographs.
- Rental-market reports.

**Implication for Jadoo:** Market-level information can help users understand localities, but Jadoo should test whether property-level cost comparison and personalized evaluation solve an unmet need.

### Housing.com

Website: https://housing.com/

**Core proposition:** Digital real-estate discovery across rental and purchase use cases.

**Relevant capabilities**
- Rental listings and search.
- Property information.
- Virtual viewing and AR/VR-related services among its broader offerings.

**Implication for Jadoo:** Virtual inspection alone is not a sufficient differentiator. Jadoo should investigate how inspection features can be integrated with cost and commute comparisons.

### 99acres

Website: https://www.99acres.com/

**Core proposition:** Property discovery across residential and commercial real estate.

**Research focus**
- Rental search and filtering.
- Listing detail quality.
- Locality information.
- Property comparison workflows.

**Implication for Jadoo:** Conduct a hands-on product audit before making claims about specific feature gaps.

## 7. Preliminary Competitive Opportunity

Jadoo's potential differentiation is the combination of multiple decision-making activities in one workflow.

| Capability | Jadoo MVP direction |
|---|---|
| Property discovery | Search and filter relevant rental listings |
| Cost transparency | Show rent, maintenance, deposit, and estimated upfront expenses |
| Property comparison | Compare shortlisted properties using standardized attributes |
| Commute evaluation | Assess travel distance or time to a specified destination |
| Virtual inspection | Support tours for properties with available 360-degree or 3D assets |
| Shortlisting | Save and revisit preferred properties |

This is a proposed positioning, not a claim that competitors lack these capabilities. Competitor feature availability must be verified through direct product testing.

## 8. Market Opportunity

Jadoo should initially target a narrow use case rather than attempt to compete with established property portals on total inventory.

The opportunity to investigate is whether renters will prefer a tool that helps them **make a decision from available listings**, rather than simply discover more listings.

Potential future opportunities include:

- Personalized property recommendations.
- Listing quality and freshness indicators.
- Neighborhood comparison.
- Budget planning for moving into a new rental.
- Owner and property-manager tools.
- Expansion to other Indian cities after validating the initial market.

These opportunities are outside the initial MVP unless user research demonstrates that one is essential.

## 9. Primary Research Plan

Secondary research identifies the market context, but direct interviews are required to understand user behavior.

### Suggested Research Sample

Interview 10-15 people who searched for a rental in Bangalore within the past 12 months. Include both successful renters and people who abandoned or postponed their search.

### Interview Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you searched for a rental home.
2. Which websites, applications, agents, or personal contacts did you use?
3. How long did it take to shortlist suitable properties?
4. Which factors mattered most: rent, deposit, location, commute, amenities, or furnishing?
5. Were there unexpected expenses or misleading listing details?
6. How many properties did you physically visit?
7. What information did you wish you had before visiting?
8. Did you compare properties using a spreadsheet, notes, or another tool?
9. Would a virtual inspection change your decision to visit a property? Why?
10. Which part of the rental search would you most want to simplify?

Avoid asking leading questions that assume the participant wants Jadoo or its proposed features.

### Research Outputs

- Ranked list of recurring customer problems.
- Common property selection criteria.
- Typical search and shortlisting workflow.
- Evidence of current workarounds.
- Feature demand and willingness-to-use findings.
- Refined target segment and MVP scope.

## 10. Key Assumptions to Validate

| Assumption | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Users struggle to compare properties | Repeated examples from interviews |
| Total cost is more useful than rent alone | User feedback and comparison tasks |
| Commute influences shortlisting | Observed decisions and interview evidence |
| Virtual tours reduce unnecessary visits | Prototype or controlled usability test |
| Users will use a separate decision platform | Repeat usage or meaningful task completion |
| Available listing data is sufficiently reliable | Audit of sample property records |

## 11. Research Limitations

- Published rental reports may reflect portal-specific searches and listing activity rather than the entire rental market.
- Asking rents do not necessarily represent final negotiated rents.
- Listing availability and pricing change over time.
- Competitor feature descriptions can change between product releases.
- A small interview sample is useful for discovering patterns but cannot establish market-wide prevalence.
- The initial demo dataset cannot establish the viability of a production marketplace.

## 13. Conclusion

Bangalore provides a relevant initial market for testing Jadoo's rental decision-making concept. Established platforms already support property discovery, so the MVP must validate a more specific value proposition: helping relocating renters understand costs, evaluate commute convenience, compare alternatives, and shortlist suitable properties with less effort.

The next research stage is to develop evidence-based user personas and map their rental search journeys.
